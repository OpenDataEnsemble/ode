ODE Formulus is the data collection instrument that hosts your own custom application and your form specifications (in jsonforms format). 
It provides bi-directional synchronization with an ODE Synkronus server. 

Notice that you need your own instance of the ODE Synkronus server for this application to be useful. 

If you want to try the application but still don't have your own Synkronus server, feel free to try out our demo app [link and instructions to be added later].
