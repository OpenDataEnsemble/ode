# Join a council

ODE is opening seats on four of its councils. You don't need to be an expert, and your contribution doesn't have to be code. We're interested in what you have contributed, what you care about, and how you would like to help the community grow.

**[Open the application form →](https://opendataensemble.org/councils/apply.html)**

## Open seats

Seats are available on these councils:

| Council | Focus |
|---|---|
| Product | Product vision, roadmap, requirements, user and implementer needs, UX/UI direction |
| Technical | Architecture, standards, APIs, security, QA and testing, releases, infrastructure |
| Community | Onboarding, mentoring, events, working groups, recognition, documentation and education |
| So.Me. | Social media, announcements, storytelling, campaigns, website content, brand and public voice |

The Governance Council has no open seats. Only existing council members can apply to it.

## Who can apply

Anyone who has shown **sustained contribution** to ODE. You judge for yourself whether you are ready. Contribution includes code and pull requests, documentation, testing, design, product feedback, answering questions on the forum, organising or helping at events, mentoring, translations and content.

If you are new to ODE, start contributing first. The [first-time contributor guide](../contribute/first-time.md) and the [forum](https://forum.opendataensemble.org/) are good places to begin, and you can apply in a later round.

## How selection works

1. **Apply** with the [application form](https://opendataensemble.org/councils/apply.html). When you finish, the form saves your answers as a text file and opens an email to hello@opendataensemble.org. Attach the file and send it.
2. **Acknowledgement.** We confirm we have received your application.
3. **Review.** Members of the council you applied to read your application. They may invite you to a short, informal conversation, or suggest a small contribution if your ODE history is short.
4. **Vote.** The Governance Council votes on new members.
5. **Reply.** Everyone gets an answer. If you are not selected this round, we suggest a next contribution so you can build toward a future round.

New members serve a **2-year term** and agree to the council member expectations: take part in council discussions and meetings, own the tasks they take on, communicate openly about their availability, and work respectfully with other contributors.

## What we look at

- **Contribution:** meaningful participation in ODE or a similar community.
- **Motivation:** an understanding of what the council is for.
- **Contribution potential:** a realistic way to contribute to that council.
- **Collaboration:** working constructively with others.
- **Commitment:** being able to take part and take responsibility.

Council membership is based on active participation rather than a fixed number of hours.
