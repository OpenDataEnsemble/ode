# Governance

ODE is an open-source, vendor-neutral project run by its community. The **ODE Governance Framework v1.0** was voted into effect by the Governance Council on 19 September 2026. The full text lives in [GOVERNANCE.md](https://github.com/OpenDataEnsemble/.github/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md).

Responsibility in ODE is earned through contribution: **eligibility through merit, selection through voting.**

## How decisions are split

- The **Product Council** decides *what* we build and *why*.
- The **Technical Council** decides *how* we build it.
- The **Community Council** looks after *people and participation*.
- The **So.Me. Council** looks after *visibility and communication*.
- The **Governance Council** looks after the rules, the council structure and the long-term health of the project.

## Roles

| Role | What it involves |
|---|---|
| **Community member** | Anyone who uses, implements or is interested in ODE. |
| **Maintainer** | Looks after the code: reviews and merges pull requests, triages issues, cuts releases, mentors contributors and makes everyday component decisions. |
| **Council member** | A voting member of a council, elected by the Governance Council, who takes part in council meetings and engages actively in ODE. |
| **Council lead** | Facilitates the council's meetings, breaks ties and delegates tasks within the council. |
| **General Secretary** | Elected from the Governance Council. Holds trademarks, funding relationships and infrastructure on behalf of the project. |

## What each council covers

Each council has **5 seats**.

| Council | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Governance** | Governance rules and charter · council structure and elections · major strategic questions · major disputes and appeals · succession · project independence · sustainability · partnerships |
| **Product** | Product vision · product roadmap · requirements · prioritisation · user needs and feedback · implementer and partner needs · UX/UI direction · product discovery |
| **Technical** | Architecture · technical standards · technical roadmap · APIs and compatibility · security · QA and testing strategy · release policy · infrastructure and CI/CD |
| **Community** | Contributor onboarding · mentoring · community events · working groups · contributor recognition · community health · developer relations · documentation and education |
| **So.Me.** | Social media · public announcements · project storytelling · campaigns and content · website and news content · brand and public voice · release and event promotion · community outreach |

## Seats

Each council has 5 seats. Members serve a 2-year term.

| Council | Seats available now |
|---|---|
| Product | Yes |
| Technical | Yes |
| Community | Yes |
| So.Me. | Yes |
| Governance | No. Open to existing council members only. |

## Join a council

Seats are open on the Product, Technical, Community and So.Me. councils. See [Join a council](./join-a-council.md) for who can apply, how selection works and the application form.

## Transparency

Governance Council minutes are published read-only. Membership decisions are recorded in the minutes and announced on the [ODE forum](https://forum.opendataensemble.org/).

Questions about governance are welcome on the [forum](https://forum.opendataensemble.org/) or at [hello@opendataensemble.org](mailto:hello@opendataensemble.org).
